# Incident Response — DRAFT, SECURITY AND LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

1. Define intake, severity, on-call roles, containment, evidence preservation, investigation, recovery and lessons learned.
2. Cover compromised accounts, payment webhook fraud, leaked content assets, malicious uploads, research data exposure and failed backups.
3. Record incident timeline, affected systems, decisions, communication approvals and restoration checks. Restrict sensitive evidence to authorized responders.
4. Notify affected parties or regulators according to applicable obligations after counsel verifies triggers and timelines.
5. Test backup restoration at the approved cadence and record recovery point/time performance.

Open decisions: contacts, notification law, severity matrix, recovery objectives, drill owner and escalation path.
