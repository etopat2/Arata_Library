# Copyright and Digital Content Licensing — DRAFT, LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

1. Identify the rightsholder and licence for each publication, edition and resource; do not assume uploaded material is cleared.
2. Define separately permitted online reading, preview, text selection, copying, printing, downloads, exports, offline use and sharing. Effective permissions can vary by publication and entitlement.
3. Explain time-limited access, device/session limits and expiry of offline licences. Accessibility accommodations must remain available.
4. Explain pseudonymous watermarking and screenshot usage notices. Browser controls cannot prevent operating-system screenshots or copying of rendered content.
5. Provide a rights complaint, correction and takedown process with an approved contact and review route.

Open decisions: licences, excerpt limits, fair-use exceptions, accessibility accommodations and takedown contacts.
